My name is Aidan Pustay. I am 20 years old and an Information Sciences and Technology (IST) major at Penn State University. This is the first mobile app and website I have made.

I created this app for two reasons. The first one is for practice with coding and app development and for something I could put on my resume to showcase my skills and talk about at a job interview.

The second reason was to fill a need (albeit a niche one). The idea for this app came to me after I participated in a casual basketball pick-up tournament one of my friends hosted. We wanted to keep track of stats and looked for an app that let us do this, but none of them suited our needs. The problem was the way we were keeping score. We were playing pick-up, so there were no free throws, and what would be typical 2- or 3-pointers in a normal basketball game were worth only 1 and 2 points, respectively. This caused issues with the apps we were trying to use. They were also just too complex for what we were trying to do. We didn't need a timer or to keep track of who was on the floor, we simply needed stats. And so that's what I made. I'm not sure how big of a need such an app is, but I know me and my friends will use it, and if other people out there, even just one person is able to use, then great.

I also made this site for two reasons. The first is to have a companion site for the app, like most apps do, where people can get Information about it. The second is to document the development process so that I can showcase my skills and work methods and what I learned while developing it and, again, give something potential employers could read at an interview.
