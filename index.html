Perfect for casual stat keeping while playing pick-up with your friends.

  • choose your scoring system (ones and twos or twos and threes)
  • choose what stats you want to keep track of (assists, rebounds, fouls, etc. are optional)
  • choose if you're playing with free throws
  • saves players and teams
  • no player minimum (can be used for a 1v1 up to a 6v6)
  • can change whose on the floor (if you choose you're playing with subs)
  • saves game history
  • and many more features
