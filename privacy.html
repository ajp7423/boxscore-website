Pickup Basketball Stat Tracker ("the App") is a basketball statistics tracking application developed by Statline Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photographs, or other personal information for the developer.

Basketball game information that you enter into the App, such as team names, player names, game statistics, scores, and game history, is used to provide the App's features.

How Your Information Is Stored

Game information is stored locally on your device so that you can use features such as game tracking and game history. The developer does not receive or maintain a copy of this information on a remote server.

How We Use Information

Information entered into the App is used only to provide and operate the App's basketball statistics and game-tracking features. We do not sell, rent, or use your information for targeted advertising.

Sharing of Information

The App does not share your game information or personal information with the developer or with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not require an account or collect personal information for the developer, there is no registration process through which the developer knowingly collects children's personal information.

Data Security

Because game information is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and its stored data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the developer's email or this site.
